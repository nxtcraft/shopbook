Free Billing App for Small Shops — Works Offline

ShopBook is a simple, free billing and inventory management app designed for small shop owners — kirana stores, grocery shops, medical stores, stationery shops, and any small business. Create bills in seconds, manage your products and stock levels, and print receipts — all without an internet connection.

How to use ShopBook

  1. Go to Products and add your items with name, price, and stock quantity.
  2. Tap New Bill and search for the products you want to add.
  3. Set quantities, enable GST if needed, and tap Finalise Bill.
  4. Print the receipt or save it as a PDF from your browser.
  5. View all past bills in History. Today's sales summary is on the Dashboard.

Key Features

  • 100% free — no subscription, no hidden cost
  • Works offline — no internet needed after first load
  • Your data stays on your device — no cloud upload, no server
  • GST billing support (any % rate)
  • Stock tracking with low-stock alerts
  • Print-ready receipts (80mm thermal printer format)
  • Sales history with today / month / all-time totals
  • No login, no signup, no app download needed

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ShopBook free?

Yes, ShopBook is completely free. No subscription, no signup, and no hidden charges. All features are available for free.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. ShopBook saves all your data in your browser's local storage. Once the page loads, everything works offline — billing, products, history. No data is sent to any server.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. ShopBook is designed with large, touch-friendly buttons and works well on any smartphone or tablet browser. No app download needed.

How do I print a receipt?

After creating a bill, a receipt will appear. Tap the Print button — it opens your device's print dialog. You can print it or save it as a PDF.

Does ShopBook support GST?

Yes. When creating a bill, toggle "Add GST" and enter the GST percentage. The app shows the subtotal, GST amount, and grand total separately on the receipt.

What happens if I clear my browser?

Your data is stored in browser local storage and will be cleared if you clear browser data. Use the Export Data button in Settings to back up your data before clearing the browser.
