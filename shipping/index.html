How to Choose the Right Courier for Your E-Commerce Orders

Shiprocket is an aggregator — it routes your shipment to the best courier (Bluedart, Ekart, Xpressbees, DTDC) based on pin code coverage. Best for small sellers who want one dashboard for multiple couriers.

Delhivery offers strong pan-India coverage especially for Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. Known for good NDR (non-delivery report) management and reliable RTO rates.

Bluedart (a DHL company) is the premium choice — highest delivery SLA compliance, best for high-value goods and B2B shipments. Rates are higher but so is the service level.

For volumetric weight: couriers use L×W×H÷5000 (cm) as chargeable weight if it's greater than actual weight. Pack tightly to avoid overpaying.
